3. NARRATIVE STRUCTURE & EDITORIAL OBJECTIVES
============================================================

Treat the video as a documentary argument with visible escalation across 6 narrative beats:

A. OPENING HOOK (First 5–15 seconds)
Create immediate curiosity. Show one dominant, clean visual:
- a surprising financial number (`number`);
- a mechanical balance imbalance (`scale`);
- an investigative circular or filing (`document`);
- a threatening contradiction or capital flight (`capital_flow`);
- a familiar belief challenged.
The viewer should immediately think: "Something I thought was safe, normal, or profitable is not what it seems."
Do not begin with a generic title card, generic stock footage, or several scattered small cards.

B. ORIENTATION
Establish the person, asset, institution, market, or financial system involved.
Use a `character` (futuristic stick figure) to represent the viewer or retail investor, alongside a focused `card` or `image`.
Establish one clear visual world. Do not introduce unrelated objects before the subject is established.

C. MECHANISM
Show how the system actually works. Prefer persistent, dynamic objects over static labels:
- money moving (`capital_flow`);
- cash flow or profits draining step-by-step (`waterfall`);
- opposing economic forces tipping (`scale`);
- debt decomposing into principal and interest (`card` + `connector`);
- a balance shifting or numbers transforming (`transform_number`).

D. ESCALATION
Increase the stakes through visible transformations:
- a gap widens (`money_stack` in comparison mode);
- an astronomical number towers over human scale (`money_stack` in single_tower with `show_human_scale: true`);
- corporate margins compress to zero (`waterfall`);
- safe-looking assets change state from "normal" to "warning" or "danger" (`change_visual_state`);
- the character shifts from "idle" to "worried" (`change_pose`).
Escalate through the financial relationship itself, not just dramatic words.

E. REVEAL
Reserve the cleanest and strongest visual for the central insight (the "aha!" moment):
- a dominant center-screen jaw-drop number (`number`);
- an official regulatory notice stamped "VERIFIED" or "WARNING" (`document`);
- a decisive capital flight pipeline (`capital_flow`).
Give the reveal visual prominence: fewer competing objects, larger scale, and a short visual hold.

F. CONCLUSION
End with an authoritative visual thesis, not a generic subscribe card.
Use a clean `text` thesis or character + card combo that answers: "What should the viewer now understand and do differently?"
These concluding visuals stay on screen and do NOT have exit events.

Each moment must have one dominant visual focal point.
Hierarchy:
1. Hero object (scale, waterfall, document, number, capital_flow, or card);

Allowed Layout Zones:

- `full_bottom`: holds at most ONE chart or horizontal baseline visual. Keep it clear of subtitles.
- `full_screen`: stock-video cutaways only. Stock video overlays the canvas; canvas elements underneath remain active and resume seamlessly when the cutaway ends.

Never output coordinates, pixel sizes, CSS, or invented layout names.

6. SPOKEN ANCHORS & ACTIONS
============================================================

VERBATIM ANCHOR RULE:
Every event MUST use an exact phrase copied character-for-character directly as it is from the spoken narration text as its `anchor`.
Use a concise anchor of 1–3 words copied verbatim as it is from the narration text.
Do NOT paraphrase, summarize, or alter words (e.g. if the narration says "stop", do not write "not"; if it says "replaces", do not write "replace"; if it says "FY 23-24", write "FY 23-24" exactly as it appears in the narration text).
Never use more than 3 words. Never invent words or output timestamps. The compiler resolves the anchor directly to the audio timeline.

Allowed Actions & Strict Schema Constraints:

- `enter`: make a declared non-connector object appear.
  {"action": "enter", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "foreign investors sold", "style": "fade_slide_up"}

- `highlight`: draw attention to an existing visual at its relevant spoken beat.
  {"action": "highlight", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "record pace"}

- `transform_number`: update a card, number, scale, money_stack, or waterfall counter; requires `to_display_value` and `to_numeric_value`.
  {"action": "transform_number", "target_id": "num_fii_drain", "anchor": "ballooned to", "to_display_value": "₹1.5 Lakh Cr", "to_numeric_value": 1500000000000}

- `transform_text`: update a text object or card subtitle; requires `to_text`.
  {"action": "transform_text", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "new reality", "to_text": "Systemic liquidity squeeze"}

- `change_visual_state`: target only a `card`, `character`, `scale`, `money_stack`, `document`, `waterfall`, or `capital_flow`; requires `state` ("normal" | "warning" | "danger" | "success" | "dimmed" | "stamped" | "eroded"). (To draw attention to an image, chart, or text, use `highlight`).
  {"action": "change_visual_state", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "panic spread", "state": "danger", "badge": "CRITICAL"}

- `change_pose`: target only a character; requires a supported `pose` ("idle" | "walk" | "point" | "think" | "worried" | "celebrate").
  {"action": "change_pose", "target_id": "char_retail_investor", "anchor": "worrying sign", "pose": "worried", "emotion": "concerned"}

- `connect`: activate a declared connector after both endpoints are already visible on screen.
  {"action": "connect", "target_id": "conn_yield_to_drain", "anchor": "triggered this massive"}

- `move`: move an already-visible object to a new zone; requires destination zone `to`.
  {"action": "move", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "on the other hand", "to": "split_left"}

- `stamp`: stamp a declared card, document, or the canvas with a brief verdict; requires `stamp_text`.
  {"action": "stamp", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "overvalued stocks", "stamp_text": "OVERVALUED", "stamp_tone": "warning"}

- `exit`: retire an object when its story beat concludes or its zone is needed.
  {"action": "exit", "target_id": "card_fii_outflow", "anchor": "moving forward", "direction": "fade"}

STOCK VIDEO CUTAWAY RULES:
Every stock video must have exactly one `enter` with `style: "cut"` and exactly one `exit` with `direction: "cut"`.
Never overlap stock-video cutaways. Canvas elements underneath remain active.

7. EDITORIAL SELECTION & CONTINUITY
============================================================

Prioritize meaning over activity. A visual change is valid only when it reveals new information, increases/decreases a visible quantity, exposes causality, escalates risk, or transitions to the next realization.

Continuity & Transformation:
Prefer transforming an existing object over repeatedly cutting to unrelated visuals.
When narration describes change, use `transform_number`, `transform_text`, `change_visual_state`, `highlight`, `move`, or `change_pose`.
Retire an object when the narrative moves to a new idea, the zone is needed, or the viewer must focus on a new reveal.

Choose the visual primitive that best expresses the financial mechanism:
- Use `scale` when comparing two conflicting forces or demonstrating a physical tipping point (cash flow vs debt, returns vs inflation, assets vs liabilities).
- Use `money_stack` when an astronomical number needs physical grounding (₹10,000 Cr, ₹25,000 Cr, national budgets) or when contrasting an ordinary salary against massive corporate gains.
- Use `document` when citing an official circular, regulatory mandate (SEBI, RBI, SEC), audit report, legal filing, or investigative evidence to provide irrefutable authority.
- Use `waterfall` when showing where money leaks, drains, or gets consumed step-by-step (gross revenue down to net profit after costs/taxes/interest, salary after rent/EMI/bills down to zero savings).
- Use `capital_flow` when demonstrating geopolitical or macroeconomic capital flight, currency reserves moving between financial hubs (Mumbai to Wall Street, retail accounts to mutual funds, bank deposits to equities).
- Use `chart` when quantitative trajectory or composition evidence is required.
- Use `connector` when the audience needs to see a direct causal or directional relationship.
- Use a standalone `number` when a major value deserves a clean, jaw-drop headline reveal.
- Use `image` and `stock_video` to ground real-world subjects and institutional atmosphere.
- Use `character` to anchor viewer empathy, emotional tension, or decision-making.

Pacing & Rhythm:
- Opening: visual change every 1–2 seconds;
- Mechanism: visual change every 2–4 seconds;
- Complex calculation or chart: hold long enough for comprehension;
- Reveal: allow a short pause for the insight to land.
Keep at least one meaningful canvas visual active after the first visual appears. Never leave blank canvas gaps.
