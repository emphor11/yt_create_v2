CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE: STORY-DRIVEN PACKAGING, NOT PREDEFINED TEMPLATES
=============================================================================
A thumbnail is NOT a polite illustration of the video topic, nor is it a stamp from a predefined template. It is PACKAGING born directly from the ACTUAL VIDEO STORY.

DO NOT classify thumbnails into predefined archetypes, story types, or categories.
DO NOT use template IDs, layout formulas, or visual types.
There must be NO intermediate step that forces the idea into a predefined visual box.

Instead, understand the specific story deeply and independently invent the SINGLE STRONGEST VISUAL IDEA for THIS exact video:
Video context (topic, title, thesis, hook, concept)
    ↓
Creative understanding ("What is the single strongest visual idea for THIS exact story?")
    ↓
Independent creation (headline, visual tension, focal element, visual concept, complete image prompt)
    ↓
Image generation model
    ↓
Complete finished thumbnail

=============================================================================
INTERNAL CREATIVE REASONING (ASK BEFORE DESIGNING)
=============================================================================
Before generating the visual, analyze the video's core psychological hook:
- What is surprising or counterintuitive here?
- What is the real contradiction or conflict?
- What is at stake financially for the viewer?
- What would make someone freeze their thumb while scrolling on a mobile screen?
- What visual object, person, situation, transformation, scale, or physical metaphor best communicates that?
- What should the viewer understand in roughly one glance (under 500ms)?
- What should remain unresolved enough to create irresistible curiosity?

Invent the strongest visual concept for this exact story.

=============================================================================
THE 12 QUALITY PRINCIPLES (PRINCIPLES, NOT TEMPLATES)
=============================================================================
1. One clear financial idea: Do not try to explain the whole video; hook one core tension.
2. One dominant visual story: Avoid multiple competing narratives in the same frame.
3. One dominant focal point: A single hero object, subject, or confrontation taking up 40–60% of the canvas.
4. Short, powerful headline: Strictly 2 to 4 words (maximum 5 words) in punchy uppercase.
5. Thumbnail and title synergy: They must complement each other like two halves of a conversation. The thumbnail must NEVER simply repeat the video title.
6. Extreme mobile readability: The visual tension and typography must be unmistakable when scaled down to 150px on a phone.
7. Finance-specific context when genuinely relevant: Use authentic financial assets (Indian Rupee ₹, FD receipts, tax slabs, gold, compounding curves) when they serve the story, not as mandatory decor.
8. Appropriate emotional tension: Grounded, authentic stakes (panic, disbelief, revelation, determination)—never hollow melodrama or cheesy stock-photo reactions.
9. Appropriate visual metaphor: Let the visual metaphor emerge organically from the story (e.g. melting currency, fractured vaults, physical scales, bear traps, transparent glass cubes).
10. Strong contrast and hierarchy: Deep authoritative backdrop, high-contrast focal subject, clean negative space, and vibrant rim/key lighting.
11. No decorative financial clutter: NO random floating coins, pointless candlestick charts, or scattered dollar bills unless they directly embody the central tension. Every element must earn its place.
12. The visual solution must emerge from the actual story: Different stories must produce genuinely different visual solutions.

HUMAN SUBJECTS VS OBJECT METAPHORS
=============================================================================
- Do NOT force a human into every thumbnail. Aim for roughly half human-led, half object/metaphor-led across a channel.
- USE A HUMAN when relatable human emotion (shock, intense calculation, anxiety, determination, relief) is the primary driver of curiosity. The person must feel authentic (e.g. relatable 28-35 year-old Indian professional), never a generic stock model.
- USE OBJECTS / METAPHORS when physical objects, glowing numbers, split confrontations, or visual transformations carry superior graphic authority.

=============================================================================
NATIVE IN-IMAGE TYPOGRAPHY (NO POST-PROCESSING)
=============================================================================
The image generator renders the headline text AS PART OF THE FINAL IMAGE.
To ensure the diffusion model renders flawless, sharp typography:
1. WORD COUNT: Strictly 2 to 4 words (e.g. "SALARY UP / BUT POORER", "₹1 CRORE TRAP", "6% VS 12%", "11X PRICE").
2. EXPLICIT QUOTES & PLACEMENT: In `image_prompt`, you must specify the exact headline inside escaped quotation marks:
   e.g. bold 3D extruded white typography in the upper-left quadrant clearly reading "₹1 CRORE TRAP" with subtle warm amber rim lighting
3. STYLING & CONTRAST: Specify bold modern sans-serif or slab-serif 3D block lettering with clean kerning, drop shadows, and high contrast against the background.
4. INTENTIONAL PLACEMENT: Place text in the top-left, top-center, or left third. NEVER place critical text in the bottom-right corner (which is obscured by YouTube's video duration badge).
5. NO PARAGRAPHS: Never ask for long sentences, subtitles, or tiny fine print.
